# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted roughly equally:

- **Firms choosing a case management system (CMS):** leadership at UK law firms (COO, managing partner, IT Director) part way through picking or replacing a CMS. They will live with the decision for years and want to know it is the right call before committing.
- **Firms already committed to a CMS:** they have chosen a platform and need it implemented quickly, with their own people involved and without a long wait to see whether it works.

## Product Purpose

Clear Rock Legal gets case management systems working with a firm's teams early. After a short discovery on one practice area, Clear Rock has that practice area running in a CMS within hours, so the firm can try it, and can compare the same practice area across different CMSs if still deciding. The site exists to get the visitor to ask to see it working (currently an email to neil@crlegal.co.uk).

## Positioning

Speed to something real. Instead of waiting months to find out whether an implementation works, the firm sees and uses its own practice area in a CMS within hours, then evolves it with Clear Rock in short loops. Clear Rock uses its own technology to turn discovery into working configuration fast; people do the judgement, technology does the repetition.

## Operating Context

- Delivery loop: **Show us** (teams walk Clear Rock through the detail) → **See it working** (within hours, hands-on) → **Evolve it together** (feedback, change, repeat).
- Testing, training, process improvement and change happen during implementation, not as separate activities at the end.
- Team includes former case handlers, paralegals, IT Directors and developers with years of CMS delivery and support experience.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page static site (`index.html`, `css/`, `js/`) served from GitHub Pages at `crlegal.co.uk`. No build step.
- Current copy is the confirmed content. Design explorations keep it as written; restructuring copy needs the user's approval.
- Primary action: "See it working →" leading to email contact.

## Brand Commitments

Binding source: `Clear Rock Legal Brand Style Guide v1.pdf` (v1.0, 25 September 2026).

- Core expression: premium, understated confidence. Disruptive in point of view, assured in execution, human in the way it works.
- Wordmark is supplied artwork (`assets/logo-saffron.svg`, `logos/`); never retype or rebuild it. Saffron R on light is the default; whiteout on dark.
- Single typeface: Inter. Sentence case.
- Palette: Olive Charcoal #293026, Warm Ivory #F5F1E8, Saffron #D8A326, Burnt Orange #C95E3F (supporting), Warm Taupe #978B80 (structural).
- Avoid: futuristic AI tropes, corporate blue, generic law imagery, decorative tech effects, gradients, glows, pill buttons, generic SaaS or consultancy feel.
- Voice: direct, human, assured; sounds like people who have lived with these systems.

## Evidence on Hand

- Illustrative CMS matter screen (fictional matter data) in the current page.
- No client names, testimonials, case studies, figures or named CMS platforms. Do not fabricate them.
- No team photography or names yet.

## Product Principles

1. Show, don't claim: a working thing beats a promise.
2. Let the proposition do the disrupting; keep the identity calm.
3. The firm's own people stay in control and are involved from day one.
4. Honest experience over polish: say when something is a bad idea.
